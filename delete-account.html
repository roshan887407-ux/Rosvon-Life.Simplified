Rosvon is built on the principle that your data belongs to you — completely and unconditionally. You can delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app, or request account deletion by email.

RECOMMENDED (METHOD 1)

📱 Delete Directly from Within the App

Delete all your cloud data, Google Drive backup, Firebase account, and local device data in one step. Deletion is instant and permanent.

  1. Open the Rosvon app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Help & Support section from the main navigation menu or drawer.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account & Data".
  4. Read the confirmation dialog showing what data will be removed.
  5. Verify your identity by re-authenticating with your account.
  6. Tap "Confirm Delete". Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately.
METHOD 2

📧 Request Account Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer email assistance, you can submit an account deletion request to our support team.

Send an email request:

📧 Email: rosvon.support@gmail.com

📌 Subject: "Account Deletion Request — Rosvon App"

📝 Include: Your registered email address and username so we can locate your account.

⏱️ Processing Time: Within 48 hours.

⚠️ Important Note on Email Deletion Requests: An email request enables us to delete your personal account data stored on our servers only (Firebase Firestore, Firebase Storage, and Firebase Authentication). Because your personal Google Drive backups and local phone data are stored privately on your own accounts and device, we cannot remotely access or delete them for you. To delete data from your phone and Google Drive, you must manually delete them using the Help & Support section inside the Rosvon app or by managing your local app data and Google account storage.

⚠️ Deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your server account and data are deleted, they cannot be recovered.

Guest Mode (Local Data) Note

If you use Rosvon in Guest (Local) Mode without an account, your data is stored only on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the app or clear storage in your device settings. This completely removes all locally stored data.
